## Profile & teaching focus
Axe smith (axes are his main focus since 2019): punching and drifting axe eyes, welding in edge steel (incl. the “hamburger technique”), mosaic and pattern-welded steel, historical axe forms. For fundamentals see “Beginner’s Guide to Forging Axes” and the fuller collaboration with Torbjörn Åhman.
