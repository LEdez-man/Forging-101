## Profile & teaching focus
UK-trained smith and teacher, author of the book series “Mastering the Fundamentals of Blacksmithing” (vol. I–III). His videos accompany the California Blacksmith Association curriculum (CBA Level I–III) and the ABANA curriculum: tapers in five cross-sections, punching/drifting theory, scarfing for forge welds, tenons and tenon shoulders, collars, grilles as test pieces. This is “extended fundamentals” in the proper sense.
