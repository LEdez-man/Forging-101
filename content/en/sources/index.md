## Modern literature (selection)
**English: standard works of the revival since the 1970s**
- Aspery, Mark: *Mastering the Fundamentals of Blacksmithing*, vols. I–III. Structured along the CBA curricula; the reference for "extended fundamentals".
- Andrews, Jack: *Edge of the Anvil*. Classic of the American revival.
- Bealer, Alex W.: *The Art of Blacksmithing* (1969, several revised editions). Cultural history and technique.
- Weygers, Alexander G.: *The Complete Modern Blacksmith*. Toolmaking from scrap steel.
- Streeter, Donald: *Professional Smithing*. Historical hardware, swage work.
- Sims, Lorelei: *The Backyard Blacksmith*. For beginners.

**German**
- Schmirler, Otto: *Werk und Werkzeug des Kunstschmieds*. Standard work on decorative techniques.
- Kühn, Fritz: *Geschmiedetes Eisen*. Design, Berlin tradition.
- Hundeshagen, Hermann: *Der Schmied am Amboß*. Textbook, available as reprint.
- Phleps: *Schmiedekunst. Ein Handwerksbuch* (1949).
- *Fachkunde Metall* / *Tabellenbuch Metall* (Europa-Lehrmittel): materials, temperatures, standards.
- *Hephaistos*: journal for metal design.

**Standards:** DIN 8580 (manufacturing processes), DIN 8583-3 (compressive forming – open-die), ISO 4063 (welding processes; 43 = forge welding), EN 10027 (steel designation).

## Schools, movements, state of the art
- **European craft tradition / decorative ironwork:** guild training into the 19th century, Art Nouveau and modernist decorative smiths (in Germany e.g. Fritz Kühn). Today: the apprenticeship Metallbauer/-in Metallgestaltung, master schools, design courses.
- **Blacksmithing revival (USA/UK, from the 1970s):** ABANA founded 1973, BABA 1978; teaching through workshops, demonstrations and later YouTube. Black Bear Forge, CCI and Mark Aspery belong to this line.
- **Scandinavian toolsmith tradition:** reference finds such as the **Mästermyr chest** (Gotland, Viking Age), which Black Bear Forge has reproduced. Torbjörn Åhman and Nils Ögren represent today's Swedish tool and axe scene.
- **State of teaching:** competency-level curricula (ABANA/CBA Levels I–III) with defined test pieces; video lessons focused on one technique at a time (BBF II, CCI *Fundamental Instruction*).
