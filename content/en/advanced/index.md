> [!note] Curricula as benchmark
> The current standard for structured training in the English-speaking world is the **ABANA curricula** (Artist-Blacksmith's Association of North America) and the **CBA Levels I–III** of the California Blacksmith Association built on them: Level I basic techniques & tools, Level II grille & toolmaking, Level III large-scale joinery and repoussé. Mark Aspery produced extensive video material for them (→ [[en/channels/mark-aspery|Mark Aspery]]). In Germany the equivalent is the apprenticeship **Metallbauer/-in, Fachrichtung Metallgestaltung** and master courses.
