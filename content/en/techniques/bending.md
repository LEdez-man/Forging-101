**Bending** changes the bar's direction without deliberately changing its section: angles, curves, eyes, rings, hooks and **scrolls**, the most important ornament of decorative ironwork.

![[assets/diagrams/biegen-en.svg|Bending – anvil edge, horn, scroll]]

## Principle
In a bend the outside is stretched and the inside compressed. Between them lies the **neutral axis**, whose length does not change. Stock calculations use it (mid-thickness):
- **Ring:** L ≈ π · (inside diameter + thickness)
- **Right angle with radius:** straight legs + ¼ · π · (2·r_inside + t)

Steel bends where it is hottest. **Heating and quenching selectively** are therefore your most important bending tools.

## Methods
- **Over the anvil edge:** let the bar overhang and strike the overhang down. Strike *beside* the edge, not right on it, or you will thin the bar there.
- **Over the horn:** curves and eyes. Worked near the tip of the horn, the curve gets tighter; near the body, wider.
- **In the vise** with a bending fork, scrolling wrench or over bending pins.
- **In jigs:** scroll forms, S-hook jigs. Indispensable for repeat work such as railings.

## Scrolls – sequence
1. **Prepare the end:** the scroll end is tapered, spread or upset depending on its form. CCI shows nine basic ends: *ribbon, snub, penny, spade, bean, fishtail, single flute, single rib, twisted*.
2. **Start the eye:** turn the tip over the anvil edge or the tip of the horn. This is the hardest part, because the eye of the scroll sets its character.
3. **Roll it in:** feed the bar further over the edge and **"knock it back"** (on the face over the part already rolled) so the scroll closes from the inside out.
4. **True it up:** flatten the scroll on the face and even out the spacing. The curve should open *steadily* (a spiral), without kinks.

## Sharp corner
A truly sharp outside corner needs extra material: [[en/techniques/upsetting|upset]] first, then bend, then forge the corner out over the edge (upset square corner).

## Common faults
| Fault | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Section dented at the bend | Struck right on the edge | Strike beside the edge, let the bar overhang. |
| Bend in the wrong place | Heated zone too long | Heat a shorter zone, cool the neighbour. |
| Scroll with kinks / flat spots | Fed unevenly | Roll in small steps, use a 1:1 drawing on the bench. |
| Scroll twisted ("propeller") | Not flattened in between | True it flat on the face after every heat. |
