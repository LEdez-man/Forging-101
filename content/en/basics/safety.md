> [!safety] Ground rule
> **Everything in the forge is hot until proven otherwise.** Steel stops glowing below about 500 °C. A bar that looks grey can still cause severe burns.

## Personal protective equipment
| Protection | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Eyes | Safety glasses to **EN 166 / ANSI Z87.1** with side shields (scale, splinters). For forge welding and gas forges, add IR protection or tinted lenses. |
| Hearing | Ear muffs or plugs. The anvil "rings" at high frequency. Damp it with a magnet or chain on the anvil and bolt the anvil firmly to its stand. |
| Clothing | **Cotton, wool or leather**. No synthetics, which melt into the skin. Long trousers without turn-ups (scale traps). Leather apron. |
| Footwear | Sturdy leather boots, ideally steel toe caps. |
| Gloves | Useful on the **tong hand**. Usually not on the **hammer hand**, where feel and grip suffer. **Never** wear gloves at rotating machines (drill, grinder). |

## Hazardous substances & air
- **Carbon monoxide (CO):** coal, coke and gas forges produce CO. Provide extraction and fresh air, and mount a **CO detector** at head height. Never run a gas forge indoors without extraction.
- **Galvanized steel:** never forge it. Zinc fumes cause **metal fume fever**. Strip the coating in dilute acid (outdoors) or use other stock. The same applies to chrome-plated, cadmium-plated and painted parts.
- **Flux (borax):** don't inhale, avoid skin contact.
- **Quench media:** oil can flare up. Keep a lid ready as a fire blanket and use no water-containing oils.

## Fire safety
- Water bucket and extinguisher (powder or CO₂) within reach
- Combustibles (sawdust, paper, solvents) out of the spark zone
- **Propane:** cylinder away from the heat, hose-burst protection, leak-check with spray. Close the cylinder valve when finished.

## Working practice
- **Tongs** must fit the section. Work that pops out of the tongs flies across the shop.
- **Offcuts** fly when hot cutting. Control where they go.
- **Order:** keep a fixed place for hot work and mark it "HOT" in chalk (CCI Pro Tip: *Mark the Cold End*).
- Don't forge when tired. Most mis-hits and injuries happen at the end of the day (CCI Pro Tip *Working Late*).
