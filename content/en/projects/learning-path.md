A practice plan that introduces each technique on a useful object. It follows the CBA Level I curriculum (Mark Aspery) and the beginner projects of Black Bear Forge and Christ Centered Ironworks.

| # | Project | New techniques | Video |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Taper bar** (5 tapers on one bar) | [[en/techniques/tapering\|Tapering]], [[en/basics/hammer-control\|Hammer control]] | [Mark Aspery – Tapers on a bar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-3ZPbxQDwY) |
| 2 | **S-hook** | Tapering, [[en/techniques/bending\|Bending]] | [Black Bear Forge – forging an S hook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_6CWRrV0S8) |
| 3 | **Fire poker** | [[en/techniques/drawing-out\|Drawing out]], eye, [[en/techniques/twisting\|Twisting]] | [Black Bear Forge – Forging a simple poker or fire rake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wktM1hd3mds) |
| 4 | **Wall hook with leaf end** | [[en/techniques/shouldering\|Shouldering]], [[en/techniques/spreading\|Spreading]], veining | [Black Bear Forge – Leaf end wall hook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v9H8oBsqykQ) |
| 5 | **Punch & hot cut** (your own tools) | Tapering, [[en/advanced/heat-treatment\|Hardening/tempering]] | [CCI – Forging a Round Punch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5ToLVNMvTvA) |

> [!tip] Learning tip
> Forge every piece **three times**: the first teaches the sequence, the second the dimensions, the third efficiency (fewer heats). Count and note your heats. The number of heats is the most honest measure of progress.
